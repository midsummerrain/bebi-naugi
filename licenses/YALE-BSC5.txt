The Bright Star Catalogue, 5th Revised Edition (Preliminary Version)
Hoffleit, D. and Warren, W. H. Jr. (1991), Yale University Observatory.
Distributed by the Astronomical Data Center (NASA) and CDS Strasbourg as catalogue V/50; in the public domain.

The hero sky uses the stars to magnitude 5.5 (positions J2000, visual magnitude, temperature), taken
from the JSON conversion published at https://github.com/brettonw/YaleBrightStarCatalog and packed by
tools/build_star_catalog.py into src/star-catalog.js.
